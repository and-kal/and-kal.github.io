Web Dev

As a web developer I like to work together with people and initiatves that are into creative uses of web technology and that like unconventional designs. But I'm also open for more traditional stuff. The important thing for me is to help folks manage their data + content themselves instead of relying on commercial third-party providers or proprietary cloud solutions. Being independent of platforms and hosting applications yourself is often not exactly easy or low-threshold for non-tech-savvy non-geeks. So I am happy to help facilitate digital sovereignty.
